04-inconsistent-dob
===================

Party:  Anand Bhat   (Person)

WHAT THIS TESTS
  Two documents from the same person giving two dates of birth.

WHAT SHOULD HAPPEN
  The reader proposes the passport's date beside the one already on the record and marks them as disagreeing. It does not choose. One of them is wrong and a person decides which.

NOTE
