## The graphs you asked about — where they are, exactly

You asked which one draws the ownership diagram. Both of these are **already

records:

**`#/report/cmp_0001` — Orion Zenith Enterprises.**
The diagram draws a **closed loop**: Orion Zenith → Pinnacle Harbor (30%) →
Dynamic Dynamic → back to Orion Zenith. The node reads *"Ownership loops
back."* The footer of the drawing reads **"Beneficial owner: more than 10% ·

**`#/report/trs_0010` — Sovereign Succession Trust.**
The diagram finds **Hannah Li at 20%** and still concludes *incomplete* —
because the **author of the trust was never declared**. The footer reads
**"Beneficial owner: 10% or more · Clause 1.3.3(d)"**.

**Put those two footers side by side. That is your whole differentiator in
one screen:** the company test is *more than* 10%; the trust test is *10% or
more*, plus the author and the trustee at any percentage at all. One
character of difference, opposite answers, and every vendor built to FATF's
25% gets both wrong.

## The five questions you will get

**"Is this real, or a mock-up?"**
Four million watchlist entities indexed on our own server in Mumbai. Live
news from GDELT. A hash-chained, append-only ledger. The only invented thing
is the demo firm's own investor book — deliberately, because you do not mix
made-up parties into a real one.

**"How is this different from ComplyAdvantage or Sumsub?"**
They sell the check. We sell the defensible record of the decision. And none
of them knows what IFSCA clause 1.3.3 says — they are all built to FATF's 25%.

**"What does the AI actually do?"**
The eight checks contain none. A model appears once, writing one paragraph of
summary, and a guard destroys that paragraph if it invents a figure. A model
may judge; only recorded facts and deterministic rules may establish.

**"Why are there no trend arrows on your dashboard?"**
Because we have no history to compute one from, and a made-up percentage on
a compliance screen is the worst defect this product could ship. Every figure
on that screen is a count of something in the record.

**"What is not finished?"**
No qualified person has verified the clause register — the product says so on
every citation. Seven document requirements could not be sourced and are
listed rather than invented. Eleven of the nineteen risk factors need a human
and cannot be read from any record. No design partner, no price, no data
licence for commercial use of the watchlist yet.

**Say that list out loud.** It is more convincing than a claim of
completeness, and it is the same honesty the product applies to itself.
